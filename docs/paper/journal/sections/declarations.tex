\section*{Data availability}
% Author decision 4 (JOURNAL_PAPER_PLAN.md section 2), closed 2026-10-23: the
% repository is made public when the paper is submitted. JSS research-data
% Option C (deposit and cite). Checked 2026-10-23: the tracked trial records
% hold pipeline outputs, labels and Yelp identifiers, and no review text.
The pre-registration (frozen at the annotated tag \code{prereg-freeze}), the
pipeline and supervisor, the injection and analysis code, every trial record and
every analysis output are publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/Hahhahahaadsa/Evaluation-as-Supervision-Journal-Edition}.
Each number in this paper is regenerated from the committed trial records by the
committed analysis scripts, without model calls. The trial records hold the
pipeline's own outputs together with the Yelp business names and the business
and review identifiers they refer to, but no review text. The Yelp Open Dataset
itself is not redistributed: it
is available from its provider under its own terms~\citep{yelpdataset}. The
copy used in this study was downloaded on 18~May~2026.
