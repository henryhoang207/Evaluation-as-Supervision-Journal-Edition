## Revised C7 design

- **Three repetitions per arm**, following the existing core's allocation:
  A1@P1 and A2@P1 on every eligible defect; A3@P0 on the same quarter stride.
  The separate two-draw subset from A10 is superseded. Before exclusions:
  142 defects, **954** trials (426 A1, 426 A2, 102 A3). The unchanged context
  inventory excludes four defects: **138 defects, 930 trials** (414, 414, 102).
- The defect remains the analysis unit. Primary exploratory estimates average
  usable repetitions within defect/arm, then compare whole defects with the
  existing bootstrap and majority McNemar. **Rep 0 alone** is an additional
  sensitivity. The A10 schema-failure sensitivity now includes all three
  repetitions. O1/severe rates, clean-output identity, collateral and paired
  Gemini comparisons remain pre-specified; Gemini comparisons now match the
  core's three-draw aggregation rather than a single local draw.
- Report stability over every primary-arm cell with three usable draws:
  identical raw response sequences and identical outcome classes, with the
  complete and planned denominators. Do not claim independent repetitions or
  bit determinism; they run contiguously on a cached local runtime.
- **G2 becomes a measurement and calibration gate.** The 8/10 identity rate
  remains a failed original gate and a reported observation. A11's readiness
  requires ten pairs and successful token calibration, plus G1/G3 and the
  unchanged G4 schema criterion. This is an explicit change, not a relabelled
  pass. Importing G2 into `C7_preflight_r3/` requires an identical runtime,
  identical selected defects and matching message hashes for all twenty calls;
  its original source report/hash are retained. Otherwise repeat the preflight
  under a fresh record rather than selecting favourable responses.
